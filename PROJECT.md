# Current project agreement

Keep the current agreement here as the project develops. Make proposed and
agreed direction distinguishable, and keep unresolved questions visible.

## Purpose and intended result

Explain the problem or opportunity, who the project serves, why it is worthwhile
and what result it aims to achieve.

## Scope and expectations

State what is included and excluded. Keep three expectations distinct:

- **Delivery acceptance:** what would make the delivered result acceptable.
- **Outcome success:** what benefit the result should produce.
- **Execution constraints:** the time, budget, resources, access and other limits
  the project must respect.

Describe how each will be assessed. Connect these expectations to relevant
[checks](tests/README.md) and [evidence](evidence/README.md).

Agree on the completion boundary: what must be accomplished before the project
can close, and which obligations may continue afterward.

## People and responsibilities

Identify who guides the direction, carries out the work, contributes specialist
knowledge and accepts or takes responsibility for the result.

Identify who may authorize consequential commitments, approve permitted
exceptions and close the project, and the limits of that authority.

## Assumptions and open questions

Record assumptions and uncertainties that could change the work. Identify
decisions needed from other people.

## Connected work

Link to [design](docs/design.md) for the approach,
[decisions](docs/decisions.md) for important choices and their reasons, and
[current work](docs/current-work.md) for the active work. Reference any applicable
standards or external agreements from their current sources.

# Evidence and supporting records

Keep selected results or references that support findings, decisions and
completion claims here. These may include measurements, observations, feedback,
check results, photographs, screenshots, exports and records from actual use.

Retain enough context to interpret the evidence: the relevant expectation or
question, conditions, source or version, date where material, observed result and
known limits. Make original observations, later interpretations and corrections
distinguishable. Useful failed attempts and missing evidence can matter too.

Link evidence from the relevant [current work](../docs/current-work.md),
[decisions](../docs/decisions.md) and [delivery record](../docs/delivery.md).
Reference originals in tools, conversations, shared drives or other storage when
practical; avoid duplicate histories and indiscriminate copies of generated output.

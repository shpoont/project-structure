# Tests and assessments

Tests are checks against project expectations, including manual assessments.
Keep the criteria, methods and materials needed to carry them out here. Cover
relevant expectations for behavior, quality, suitability, delivery acceptance,
outcome success and execution constraints.

Choose methods appropriate to the question: inspections, calculations,
experiments, expert reviews, participant feedback, comparative assessments or
automated tests. Reviews and evaluations belong here alongside other checks.

Connect each check or assessment to the relevant
[project expectations](../PROJECT.md) or [approach](../docs/design.md). Record
the question, criteria, conditions, expected outcomes and known limits. Explain
how to carry it out and interpret the result, what evidence is needed, when to
check and who is responsible. Include tools or commands when needed.

Identify which checks form each piece of work's
[implementation contract](../docs/design.md#implementation-contract) and when
each check must pass. Checks that need delivery or actual use remain pending
until valid, applicable evidence is available.

Before a costly or consequential action, identify its required checks and
confirm that material decisions have been sufficiently reviewed and other
prerequisites are ready. A required check without a valid, applicable pass
blocks the action it governs unless an authorized decision-maker explicitly
approves a permitted exception within the project's agreed limits, applicable
obligations and that person's authority. Record any exception with the relevant
[decision](../docs/decisions.md), including its justification, permitted action,
remaining risk and any conditions or follow-up. An exception does not change
the check's result.

Prepare relevant checks before the work they will guide, and carry them out
when the necessary evidence is available. Use measurable criteria where
meaningful; otherwise describe observable criteria and an explicit assessment
method. Check that the method can support the conclusion being drawn, including
acceptable alternatives, misleading positive results and missing evidence
where relevant.

Keep prepared checks, observations, interpretations and uncertainty distinct.
Record whether each check passed, failed or remains pending, with any necessary
follow-up. Link retained results and their limits through
[evidence/](../evidence/README.md), and reference shared criteria, procedures and
materials rather than duplicating them.

# Decisions

Record important choices as they are made, with enough context to understand why:
the question, decision, reasons, relevant alternatives, consequences and affected
work. Retain who or what established the decision and when where that matters.

Make proposals, accepted decisions and superseded decisions distinguishable.
When a decision changes, preserve its earlier reasoning and explain what changed.
Link to the affected [agreement](../PROJECT.md), [design](design.md), checks and
supporting evidence, then update their current descriptions.

A short entry can cover the useful context. Link to decisions already retained
in another system rather than copying their full history. Split into separate
records only when that improves navigation.

# Design and approach

Explain how the project will achieve the result agreed in
[PROJECT.md](../PROJECT.md). Begin with the whole project and develop detail as
the work needs it.

## Approach and important parts

Describe the proposed approach and its important parts: activities, workstreams,
deliverables, processes or components. Explain how they work together, their
dependencies and who is responsible for them.

## Connections and open questions

Connect design choices to the expectations they serve. Link to relevant
[element detail](elements/README.md), [mocks](../mocks/README.md),
[checks](../tests/README.md) and [working materials](../src/README.md). Identify
assumptions, unresolved choices and aspects that need exploration or assessment.

## Implementation contract

Before implementing each piece of work, agree on the tests it must satisfy to
count as implemented. This is its implementation contract. Cover the relevant
expectations, quality, constraints and interactions of the real parts.

Prepare the [checks](../tests/README.md), including what a pass looks like and
what evidence is needed. Record the agreement here or link to it, keeping the
design and its reasons alongside the checks. Link the contract from the relevant
[current work](current-work.md).

Identify checks that need delivery or actual use, with responsibility and
timing. Include any ability to collect their evidence in the implementation
contract when it must be built into the result.

## Findings and changes

Keep the current design understandable. Retain important change reasons in
[decisions.md](decisions.md) and use findings from checks and actual use to
update affected parts coherently.
